How it works

Three easy steps.

No complicated process. Just enough information to make a safe, thoughtful connection.

1

2

3

Meet, then walk

Safety & Trust

Kindness works best with clear expectations.

Walk With Me is designed to make volunteering simple while still protecting dog owners, volunteers, and dogs.

First Walks

Before the first walk, we'll spend a few minutes meeting you and your dog, reviewing the leash setup, routine, commands, triggers, and anything else we should know. If everyone is comfortable, we'll head out for the walk from there.

Honest Dog Information

Owners should disclose known bite history, aggression, reactivity, escape tendencies, and anything else that could affect a safe walk.

Walk Check-Ins

Volunteers should confirm when a walk begins and when the dog has been safely returned.

Emergency Contacts

Each match should have a reliable owner contact and an alternate emergency contact available during the walk.

Know Your Limits

Volunteers should only accept dogs and situations they feel physically and confidently able to handle.

What We Are — and Aren't

Walk With Me provides volunteer dog walking. It is not veterinary care, medical assistance, transportation, pet sitting, or emergency response.

Privacy

We collect only information needed to coordinate walks, communicate with participants, and respond to safety concerns. We do not ask for medical records or proof of disability. Participants should avoid sending highly sensitive personal, financial, or medical information.

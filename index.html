About Walk With Me

Built around dogs, neighbors, and practical kindness.

An injury, disability, surgery, or mobility challenge can make a normal dog walk difficult. We make it easier for someone who needs help to connect with someone nearby who is willing to give it.

For Owners

Ask for a little help.

Get support when walking your dog is difficult because of injury, recovery, disability, or limited mobility.

For Volunteers

Give a little time.

Choose walks that fit your schedule, your comfort level, and your experience with dogs.

For Community

Make a real difference.

One simple walk can mean fresh air for a dog and peace of mind for someone who needs it.

How it works

Three easy steps.

No complicated process. Just enough information to make a safe, thoughtful connection.

1

Reach out

Tell us about your dog, your neighborhood, and the kind of help you need.

2

Find a match

We connect owners and volunteers based on location, schedule, and comfort with the dog.

3

Take the walk

Meet safely, agree on the details, and give the dog some fresh air and exercise.
